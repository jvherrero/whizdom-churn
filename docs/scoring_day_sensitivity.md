# Scoring-Day Sensitivity

The backtest scores on the first day of each month. Here the registered LightGBM of each brand also scores days 1, 8, 15, 22, 29 of its test months, with no new training, and each day is judged on its own 60-day label against the recency rule on the same players. Only days on or after the first test cutoff of the model and with a closed label. Generated by `src/models/scoring_day_sensitivity.py` (`make scoring-days`).
