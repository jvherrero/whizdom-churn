Notes

  • There is no `session` table under org/10-landing/kafka-sink for either operator (confirmed by listing both operators' prefixes in full). This profile is derived from `bet` (whizdomai-transactions) instead: a new session starts after 30 minutes with no bet event for that player. That threshold was chosen by inspecting the real distribution of gaps between consecutive bets (see 01_profiling.ipynb) -- 99.81% of all such gaps are under 30 minutes, and the distribution has no sharp elbow, so this is a reasonable choice, not a uniquely provable one.
  • Unlike the other 4 tables, this profile only covers the 2026-09-23 to 2026-09-29 window, not the full available history -- computing sessions over ~104 days would cost as much as the 7-day version already does (~18 minutes for both operators), scaled up roughly 15x.
  • The key is a synthetic composite (operator + partyId + session_id), built for this check only -- dq_lib's KeyCheck covers a single column, and no single raw column identifies a session on its own.
  • The date range above goes one day past QUALITY_END (2026-09-29): S3 partitions are by UTC calendar day, but `dateTime` is stored in local time (+02:00). Confirmed directly against S3: the 2026-09-29 partition holds timestamps from 2026-09-29 01:59:59+02:00 to 2026-09-30 01:59:59+02:00 -- about 10.9% of that day's rows (1.5M of 13.8M in primus alone) fall on local date 2026-09-30. This profile's date range reflects the real event timestamp (local time), not the partition folder, unlike the other 4 tables -- which is why it can extend one day beyond the partition window we read.
