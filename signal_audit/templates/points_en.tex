\section{Evaluation Criteria}\label{sec:criteria}
Each column has its checks in \code{signal_checks.yaml}; they run on the snapshots of the formulas, with the latest version of each row. A check \emph{passes} if it holds in at least 99\% of the rows; it is \emph{almost} from 95\% to 99\%, and it \emph{fails} below that. The tolerance $0.01 + 0.001\,|f|$ lets the rounding of the amounts pass. Each point also has its own rule (table of section~\ref{sec:summary}), calculated with recomputed data (each bet, the daily gaming table) or with the impossible values. A column is \stWrong{} if the point that explains it fails, \stAffected{} if the point of one of its inputs fails, \stUnconfirmed{} if the workbook does not give the definition, and \stCorrect{} if it passes all its checks.
